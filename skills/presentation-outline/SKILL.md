# Presentation Outline

Turn a topic and supporting materials into a presentation-ready narrative.

## Workflow

1. Identify the audience, duration, presentation goal, expected slide count, team size, source materials, and optional output path.
2. Read relevant workspace files when supplied. Do not invent evidence, figures, or citations.
3. Establish one central takeaway and a narrative arc: hook, problem, analysis, evidence, conclusion, and action or discussion.
4. Follow [assets/outline-template.md](assets/outline-template.md), adapting slide count to the time limit.
5. Give each slide one job. Include concise on-slide content, visual suggestions, speaker-note prompts, and estimated time.
6. Check that total timing fits and transitions are explicit. For group work, assign clear ownership.
7. Save requested Markdown output only through an approved Workspace Update Tool.

Prefer a usable speaking structure over dense paragraphs copied onto slides.
